# KumbhOps AI

### AI-Powered Volunteer Deployment & Emergency Response Command Center

KumbhOps AI is an intelligent workforce management platform designed for large-scale events such as **Simhastha Kumbh**. The platform streamlines volunteer recruitment, approval, assignment, task tracking, and emergency response through AI-assisted decision-making.

---

## Problem Statement

Managing thousands of volunteers during large public events is a complex challenge.

Authorities must:

* Recruit and verify volunteers
* Match volunteers with suitable roles
* Allocate workforce across multiple zones
* Handle emergencies in real time
* Monitor task progress and workforce availability

Traditional manual processes are slow, inefficient, and difficult to scale.

---

## Solution

KumbhOps AI provides a centralized AI-powered command center that enables authorities to:

* Approve volunteers efficiently
* Generate intelligent role recommendations
* Assign volunteers to operational zones
* Track task progress
* Manage emergency situations
* Monitor workforce utilization

The platform combines AI recommendations with human oversight for better operational decision-making.

## AI Workflow

1. Volunteer registers.
2. AI evaluates skills, experience, training, and preferences.
3. Manager approves volunteer.
4. AI generates role and zone recommendations.
5. Assignment is issued.
6. Volunteer accepts or rejects the task.
7. Task progress is tracked.
8. Assignment is completed and archived.
9. Volunteer becomes available for reassignment.
